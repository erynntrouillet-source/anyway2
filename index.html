Our story

It started with a sore shoulder.

On a holiday, one of us carried a tote bag all day and ended up with an aching shoulder. A backpack would have been easier on the body, but most backpacks are not made to be seen with, especially for women.

Tote bags look good and hurt after an hour. Backpacks carry well and rarely look good. Students should not have to pick one, and they should not have to pay a lot to avoid it.

So we made Anyway: a bag that keeps the look of a tote and spreads the weight like a backpack.

Three things we would not give up.

It looks like a tote

A shape you would carry to dinner, in calm neutral tones. Nobody reads it as a hiking bag.

It carries like a backpack

Straps and attachment points borrowed from travel backpacks, so the weight is shared instead of sitting on one shoulder.

It stays affordable

We left out extra accessories and complicated mechanisms, which keeps the bag light and the price within a student budget.
